• A dictation made in the app is no longer lost if Android closes the app.
• Keyboard: height fitted to the screen, a single bar as on iOS, undo for an autocorrection, a layout per language, no autocorrection in e-mail addresses and URLs.
• New onboarding: pick your language, guided first dictation.
• Display fixed in landscape, on small screens and with a large font.
