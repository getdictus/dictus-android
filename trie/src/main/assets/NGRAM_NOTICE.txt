Dictus Android n-gram assets

French and English NGRM assets are derived from the Google Books Ngram Corpus Version 3 and the orgtre/google-books-ngram-frequency extracts.

Source: https://github.com/orgtre/google-books-ngram-frequency
Original dataset: https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv3.html
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode

Dictus filtered, normalized, added Dictus-authored seed pairs, capped results, normalized scores, and converted the source frequencies to the NGRM v1 binary format. Exact revisions, hashes, transformations, and reproduction commands are documented in docs/ngram-assets-provenance.md.
