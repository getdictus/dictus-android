Dictus Android language assets

N-gram assets (fr/en/es/de_ngrams.dict) are derived from the Tatoeba sentence corpus.

Source: https://tatoeba.org
Downloads: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language
License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/legalcode
Snapshot: 2026-08-30

A part of the Tatoeba corpus is additionally available under CC0 1.0.

Spell-check assets (es/de_spellcheck.dict) are derived from the Google Books Ngram
Corpus Version 3 and the orgtre/google-books-ngram-frequency extracts.

Source: https://github.com/orgtre/google-books-ngram-frequency
Original dataset: https://storage.googleapis.com/books/ngrams/books/datasetsv3.html
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/legalcode

Dictus segmented, normalized and counted the Tatoeba sentences, filtered and normalized
the Google Books frequencies, and converted both into its DTRI v1 and NGRM v1 binary
formats. N-gram results were capped and score-normalized. French and English include
Dictus-authored seed pairs; Spanish and German have no authored seeds. Exact revisions,
hashes, transformations, and reproduction commands are documented in
docs/ngram-assets-provenance.md.
